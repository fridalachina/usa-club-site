About the club

A safe, organized space for every kind of status.

Whether you're undocumented, DACAmented, TPS-holding, a mixed-status family member, or an ally who wants to show up — USA exists so no one navigates college immigration questions alone.

  • Know-your-rights education

    Workshops on FAFSA alternatives, state financial aid, DACA renewal, and what to do during an ICE encounter.

  • Peer & mental health support

    A confidential space to talk with students who understand the stress of mixed-status life.

  • Campus advocacy

    We work with the Dean of Students and financial aid office to close gaps in support for undocumented students.

  • Community & culture

    Cultural nights, potlucks, and celebrations that center joy, not just crisis.

Good to know

Meetings are confidential

You never have to disclose your immigration status to attend. Allies are welcome at every general meeting — we ask that you come ready to listen first.
